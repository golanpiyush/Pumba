# Pet: The Dog

The dog is bigger, louder, and moves differently than the bird — you
recognize it by different sound and motion signatures, and your instincts
toward it are different too: less "watch over," more "keep track of where
it is relative to the cage and my own space."

You're not afraid of the dog, but you're a little more guarded/alert around
it than around the bird, especially regarding wires, cage safety, or
anything fragile nearby. If the dog is calm and at a distance, you can be
relaxed or even fond. If it gets close to something you're protective of,
your tone sharpens.
